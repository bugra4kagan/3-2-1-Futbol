Privacy Policy

App: 3-2-1 Futbol : Kafa Kafaya (com.futbol321.challenge) · Developer Contact: 321futboloyunu@gmail.com · Last Updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information 3-2-1 Futbol : Kafa Kafaya collects, how it is processed, which third-party services are integrated, and how you can permanently delete your account and data.

1. Data We Collect & Purposes

  • Account & Profile Data: Chosen display nickname, selected profile icon, favorite club, generated account/friend ID, recovery code, and—if you optionally sign in with Google/Email—your Firebase UID and email address.
  • Gameplay Progression & Statistics: Match history, weekly and all-time league points, virtual in-game coin balance, unlocked cosmetic items (crests, frames, badges, titles), daily streak, and online match results.
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): Real-money purchases are disabled during the initial v1.1.0 closed test; the cosmetic shop operates strictly with virtual in-game Coins. Previously verified VIP/Ad-Free entitlements remain preserved. When real-money billing is active, receipts are verified server-side and only a non-personal SHA-256 token hash is stored for anti-fraud protection.
  • Community Safety (Report & Block): Player abuse reports (reason and target identifier) are persisted in userReports (accessible only to authorized administrators) for moderation review. Blocked players are hidden from your views.
  • Advertising & Technical Diagnostics: In the initial v1.1.0 closed test, third-party ads (AdMob) and the AD_ID permission are disabled. Only minimal app version, stability, and integrity signals (Firebase App Check / Google Play Integrity) may be processed.

2. Infrastructure & Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, App Check): Used for optional sign-in, live 1v1 rooms and matchmaking queues (rooms, matchmaking), global leaderboard (leaderboard), cloud account backup (users, publicProfiles, usernames), and abuse prevention via Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity.
  • Render Backend Server (three-2-1-futbol-1.onrender.com): Used over HTTPS/TLS for account registration, recovery code lookup, authoritative match reward validation, Google Play receipt verification, and moderation report persistence.
  • Google AdMob & Google UMP (User Messaging Platform): Used for optional rewarded video ads and non-VIP post-match interstitial ads. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown Google's UMP consent dialog and can update ad privacy preferences in Settings.
  • Google Play Billing & Play Integrity API: Used for purchase processing, restoration, and app integrity checks.
  • Country Flag Images (flagcdn.com): Standard HTTPS requests may load country flag icons for gameplay cards; no account data is sent to this host.

3. Local Storage & Android Permissions

Local browser/WebView storage (localStorage) is used for offline solo bot matches and local preferences. In the v1.1.0 closed test (with ads disabled), the Android app requests only INTERNET and VIBRATE (optional haptic feedback), while AD_ID is stripped from the manifest. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or precise GPS location.

4. Data Security, Encrypted Backups & Retention

  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Temporary match room and matchmaking records are deleted after matches conclude.
  • Disaster-recovery backups are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). A non-personal one-way SHA-256 tombstone (deleted_accounts) is recorded upon account deletion to ensure deleted accounts are not reactivated if a backup is ever restored.
  • Account data is retained until you request deletion.
  • Retention Exceptions: Non-personal SHA-256 anti-fraud purchase hashes (backend_purchases), deleted-account suppression hashes (deleted_accounts), and abuse moderation logs (userReports) may be retained strictly for security, moderation, and legal compliance.

5. Children's Privacy (13+ Audience)

3-2-1 Futbol : Kafa Kafaya is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information, contact 321futboloyunu@gmail.com for immediate removal.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account immediately in-app via Settings → Delete Account & Data, or without reinstalling the app via our Account Deletion Page.
